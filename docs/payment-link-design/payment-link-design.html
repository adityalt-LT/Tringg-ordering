Payment link design

What a caller receives after a phone order: four texts, and the payment page the link opens. The page shows the restaurant’s name, never Tringg’s. Card details are entered on Stripe’s fields. The link is valid for 15 minutes.

Text messages

1 · Right after the call
ROSSA Pizzeria: Thanks Mike. Your order is ready to pay: 2 items, $28.55. Pay by 7:42 PM (15 min): pay.tringg.ai/r/8KQ2
You can add a tip on that page.
2 · Once paid
ROSSA Pizzeria: Paid $28.55. Order #1042. Pickup at Wicker Park in about 20 min. Receipt: pay.tringg.ai/r/8KQ2
3 · If the link expires unpaid
ROSSA Pizzeria: Your payment link for order #1042 expired, so we cancelled the order. Call (312) 555-0100 to order again.
4 · If the restaurant can’t make it
ROSSA Pizzeria: We couldn’t make order #1042, so you’ve been refunded $28.55. It can take 5–10 days to reach your card.
